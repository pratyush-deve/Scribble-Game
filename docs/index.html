1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Scribble ("the App"), a multiplayer real-time drawing and word-guessing game for Android developed by Pratyush Nishank ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how that information is stored and processed on your device and on Google Firebase infrastructure, how long it is retained, and how you can exercise your privacy and data deletion rights.

Scribble does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or data brokerage services. All data collected is used strictly to provide authentication, real-time multiplayer gameplay, player profiles, and match statistics.

2. Information We Collect & Process

2.1 Account & Profile Information

  • Firebase User Identifier (UID): Every user—whether signing in with Google or playing as a guest—is assigned a unique Firebase Authentication user ID (uid) used to associate rooms, drawings, scores, and profile records.
  • Google Sign-In Information (Permanent Accounts): When you sign in or link an account using Google Sign-In (via AndroidX Credential Manager and Google Identity Services), we receive your Google ID token, display name, and email address via Firebase Authentication. Your profile document in Cloud Firestore stores your uid, chosen displayName (1–25 characters), selected emoji avatarId, accountType (PERMANENT), timestamps (createdAt, updatedAt, lastSeenAt), and your email if you upgraded from a guest account.
  • Guest Accounts: When you choose to play as a Guest, the App authenticates you via Firebase Anonymous Authentication and creates a profile document with accountType: "GUEST", your chosen display name, avatar selection, and guest eligibility counters (guestMatchesCompleted and guestAccessStatus).
  • User Preferences: Preferences such as sound settings (soundEnabled) are stored within your profile document.

2.2 Multiplayer Gameplay & Room Data

  • Room & Lobby Metadata: When you host or join a game room, your uid, display name, selected avatar, host status, connection presence (isConnected), and current match score are stored in Firebase Realtime Database under /rooms/{roomId} so other participants in the same room can play with you.
  • Live Drawings: While you are the active drawer, vector drawing strokes (normalized coordinates, color, stroke width, tool type, and sequence numbers) are transmitted in real time through Firebase Realtime Database under /rooms/{roomId}/drawing/{turnId}/strokes.
  • Guesses & In-Game Chat: Text messages and word guesses submitted during a game are stored in /rooms/{roomId}/chat alongside your senderId, senderName, and timestamp so room participants can view the live chat and guess feed.
  • Turn & Word Selection State: Word choices for the active drawer are stored in an isolated database path (/gameSecrets/{roomId}/{drawerId}) accessible only to that drawer, while the public room state holds only the masked hint and a SHA-256 hash of the selected word.
  • Replay Voting: At the end of a match, if players vote to "Play Again", boolean votes keyed by player uid are processed by our Cloud Function (submitPlayAgainVote) under /rooms/{roomId}/replayVoting.

2.3 Match History & Lifetime Statistics

  • Completed Match Records: When a multiplayer match with at least 2 players finishes, our server-side Cloud Function (finalizeMatchStats or finalizeReplaySession) records an authoritative match summary in Cloud Firestore at /matches/{matchId} containing participant UIDs (playerUids), final scores, winner UID, draw status, round count, draw time setting, and completion timestamp.
  • Lifetime Player Statistics: For permanent accounts, the backend atomically updates aggregate statistics in your Firestore user profile (gamesPlayed, wins, losses, draws, totalScore, bestScore, and winStreak).

3. Guest Access, Eligibility & Account Linking

Scribble allows users to try the game as an anonymous guest for up to 2 completed multiplayer matches. After each completed match, the backend increments guestMatchesCompleted on the guest profile and sets guestAccessStatus to EXHAUSTED once 2 matches are reached.

When a guest links a Google account:

  • If the Google account is not yet linked to an existing Scribble user, the guest account is upgraded in place to a PERMANENT account, preserving the same uid, display name, and avatar.
  • If the Google account is already associated with an existing permanent Scribble account, the App safely signs you into your existing permanent account without overwriting or deleting either record.

5. Android Permissions

Scribble requests only the minimum permission required for online multiplayer operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Required to communicate with Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise/coarse location, photo library, or external storage.

6. Data Retention & Automatic Cleanup

We distinguish between temporary real-time gameplay data and persistent profile/match records:

  • Temporary Room, Drawing & Chat Data (Realtime Database):
    • Drawing strokes and secret word choices are cleared between turns or when a match restarts/cleans up.
    • When a host leaves a room, or when post-match replay voting fails or expires, the room code mapping (/roomCodes/{code}), secret state (/gameSecrets/{roomId}), and room node (/rooms/{roomId})—including all in-room chat messages and drawing strokes—are deleted.
    • A scheduled Cloud Function (checkReplayTimeouts) runs every 1 minute to automatically close and delete finished rooms whose replay voting deadline has expired, rooms closed for more than 20 seconds, or finished rooms abandoned without activity for more than 2 minutes.
  • Abandoned Guest Accounts (30-Day Scheduled Cleanup):
    • A scheduled Cloud Function (cleanupAbandonedGuestAccounts) runs every 24 hours and automatically deletes anonymous GUEST user profiles in Cloud Firestore (/users/{uid}) and their corresponding Firebase Authentication user record if lastSeenAt is older than 30 days and the user is not in an active room.
    • Historical match summary documents in /matches/{matchId} are preserved so other participants' match history remains intact.
  • Permanent Accounts & Match Records:
    • Permanent user profiles (/users/{uid}) and completed match records (/matches/{matchId}) are retained for as long as your account remains active, unless you request deletion (see Section 7).

7. Your Rights & How to Request Account / Data Deletion

You can update your display name and avatar at any time directly inside the App via the Profile & Customization screen, or sign out of your session using the Sign Out button.

Because direct client-side deletion of Firestore user profiles and match records is disabled in our security rules (allow delete: if false) to prevent unauthorized tampering, permanent account and data deletion requests are handled by the developer upon request:

  • How to Request Deletion: Send an email to pratyush.deve@gmail.com with the subject line Scribble Account Deletion Request from the Google email address associated with your Scribble account (or include your display name / user ID if requesting deletion of a guest record).
  • What Will Be Deleted: Upon verifying ownership of the account, we will delete your Firebase Authentication account and your Cloud Firestore profile document (/users/{uid}), and remove or anonymize your identifier in associated records within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 30 days).

8. Security Measures & Limitations

We implement multiple technical safeguards to protect player data:

  • All network traffic between the Android client and Google Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Firestore Security Rules enforce strict authentication and ownership checks (for example, users can only read and update their own /users/{userId} profile, cannot manipulate their own lifetime statistics or guest match counters, and can only read /matches/{matchId} documents in which they participated).
  • Target words during gameplay are protected by isolated security rules on /gameSecrets and SHA-256 hashing on public game state.

Please note that while we take reasonable measures to secure your data, no method of electronic transmission or cloud storage is 100% immune to security risks. Keep your device and Google account credentials secure.

9. Service Providers & International Data Processing

Scribble relies on Google Firebase (Google LLC) for authentication, database hosting, and serverless Cloud Functions. Our Cloud Functions are configured to execute in the us-central1 (United States) region, and Firebase services may process data on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States or other regions where Google operates. For more information on how Google processes data, please review the Privacy and Security in Firebase documentation and Google's Privacy Policy.
